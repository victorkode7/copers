Copers Sans is a modified version of Pixeloid Sans by GGBotNet.
It remains licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; see
pixeloid-sans-license.txt for the original copyright notice and full license.
The modified font is renamed because "Pixeloid" is a Reserved Font Name.

Modification: extend each arm of U+002B PLUS SIGN by one original pixel,
preserving stroke thickness and the vertical center. Increase only the plus
advance width by two pixels to preserve space before the following character.
All other glyph outlines, character widths, and line metrics are unchanged.
Both regular and bold weights are modified. Original fonts are retained.

Rebuild from the project root:
powershell -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File scripts/enlarge-plus.ps1
