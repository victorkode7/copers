# World progression design intent

## Summary

As of 2026-11-08, the default for world progression is **make enemies stronger,
unless the owner states otherwise**. Enemy weaknesses and other strategic
mechanics are tentative ideas for later.

This records owner direction. It does not change enemies or verify the current
implementation.

## Current default

Follow the make-enemies-stronger rule when considering world progression.
The owner has not selected particular stats, numerical increases, or scaling
formulas in this statement.

Evidence: [[raw/2026-11-08/batch-03/world-progression-design-intent.txt|Owner reply]], line 1.

## Ideas to keep in mind

The owner has considered a mix of enemies, possible weaknesses, or other ways
for the player to strategize. The proposed strategic direction is described
as unimplemented and remains undecided.

If pursued, it might use something other than skill-tree or equipment changes.
No specific weakness system, build requirement, or strategy mechanic is chosen.
These possibilities do not replace the current stronger-enemies default.

Evidence: [[raw/2026-11-08/batch-03/world-progression-design-intent.txt|Owner reply]], line 1.
