# Crafting design intent

## Summary

Working direction recorded on 2026-11-07: Crafting mainly provides a way to
make items early in the game. There is no current pity-system plan. The owner
prefers limited growth of ordinary monster materials beyond World 2, while
exclusive recipes and boss/special-area materials remain possible.
The ordinary role of Crafting after World 2 is not yet settled. Large quantities
of existing materials are another tentative recipe option.

## Owner-stated direction

| Topic | Status | Direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Early game | Stated role | Provide a way to make items |
| Pity system | No current plan | Do not assume a guaranteed remedy for unlucky drops |
| Bad-luck fallback | Not currently favored | Probably not Crafting's main role |
| Ordinary mob materials past W2 | Preference | Avoid adding more routinely |
| Exclusive-material recipes | Possibility | Some items might use exclusive materials |
| Boss/special-area materials | Possible exceptions | May add materials for bosses or areas outside regular maps |
| Large quantities of existing materials | Tentative example | A later recipe might use 100k fangs; no actual cost or recipe is chosen |

## Scope and uncertainty

The earlier message's post-World-2 clause is unfinished. The material preference
does not establish that Crafting stops after World 2, nor define its exact
later-game role. The owner has now explicitly floated continued use of established
materials in large amounts. This remains an option, not a settled later-game system.

The material discussion concerns monster materials in item creation. Town
resources are a separate system in [[wiki/Foundations/Game-Glossary]]; these replies do not
define whether that resource catalogue should expand.

## Open questions

- Which later recipes, if any, should use large amounts of established materials,
  and what costs would suit them? The 100k-fang example does not decide either.
- How should item drops and Crafting divide the later equipment progression?
- Which exclusive recipes, boss drops, or special areas might be worth adding?
- What makes those recipes worthwhile without relying on a pity-system role?
