# Crafting materials and pity-system direction

## Provenance

Owner reply captured on 2026-11-07. The UTF-8 snapshot preserves the message
wording with a terminal newline; the hash above identifies it.
[[raw/2026-11-07/batch-01/crafting-materials-direction.txt|Original reply]], line 1.
This clarifies the discussion following [[wiki/Sources/Source-Crafting-Early-Game-Intent]].

## Summary

There is no current plan for a pity system, so Crafting is probably not intended
as a fallback for items that refuse to drop. Exclusive-material recipes are
possible, but the owner prefers avoiding additional regular monster materials
past World 2. Bosses and areas outside the regular maps may be exceptions.

## Findings and evidence

- No pity-system plan exists as of this statement; this is not a permanent ban.
- Crafting as a bad-luck fallback is not the currently favored role.
- Some items might use exclusive materials; no recipes or exclusivity rules are selected.
- The owner is reluctant to add more ordinary mob materials past World 2.
- Additional boss materials or materials from special areas outside regular maps
  are possibilities, not confirmed content additions.
