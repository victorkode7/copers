## Summary

Town's working direction is an optional side mechanic that helps adventure
progression and offers meaningful investment choices. The owner explicitly
describes the feature as not fully realized and the possible mechanisms as
largely undecided.

## Findings and evidence

- Paragraph 1: Town should help progression without forcing participation;
  monster mastery is the owner's example of optional acceleration.
- Paragraph 2: the owner currently experiences a linear wait-and-buy path and
  wants production investment versus immediate player unlocks/buffs, sometimes
  sacrificing Town growth. Cross-system feedback is worth exploring.
- Paragraph 2: the owner names Forge and Managerie as examples. Forge's referent
  was unresolved in this message; [[wiki/Sources/Source-Town-Terminology]] records the
  later clarification. Preserve the original wording as historical evidence.
- Paragraph 3: monster-material building costs are a conditional idea that
  could make Menagerie/mastery more valuable, not a confirmed current cost.
- Paragraph 4: the goal is problem solving rather than a chore or waiting loop;
  individual mechanics remain undecided.
