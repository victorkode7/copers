# Owner's build balance boundaries

## Provenance

Owner reply captured verbatim from this conversation on 2026-11-08 in batch-02,

melts bosses could cross the line despite specific equipment and tradeoffs.

Evidence: [[raw/2026-11-08/batch-02/build-balance-boundaries.txt|Original reply]], line 1.
This clarifies [[wiki/Sources/Source-Build-Design-Intent|the earlier build direction]];
it does not supersede it or establish a measured balance result.

## Summary

Acceptable build power depends on the content. Excelling at one task can be
fine. Intentionally difficult content should still reward planning or strategy,
and a build suited to that challenge need not also be best at everything else.

## Findings and evidence

- In response to the boss-melting example, the owner says that would not cross
  the line. A build being very good at one thing is probably acceptable when
  it is less effective at others, subject to the content in question.
- Feeling overpowered alone is not the owner's concern.
- For content intended to be hard or require planning, the owner would consider
  reviewing a build that breezes through without needing any adjustment.
  This is a reason to investigate, not an automatic nerf decision.
- A build suited to very hard content may remain good elsewhere; the owner
  distinguishes that from being the best at everything.
- A dungeon mechanic, future potions, build changes, and unique-weapon choices
  are examples of possible content/preparation. This reply does not commit to
  adding those systems or requiring a particular preparation method.

## Open questions

No numerical threshold, content-specific success criterion, forced build-swap
rule, or particular rebalance is selected. Future hard content needs its own
challenge goals; the dungeon and potion examples do not specify mechanics.
