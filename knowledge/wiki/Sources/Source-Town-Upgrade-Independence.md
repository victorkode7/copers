# Owner's Town upgrade independence rule

## Provenance

Owner reply captured verbatim on 2026-11-08 in batch-04, using UTF-8 with one
terminal newline. The SHA-256 above identifies the snapshot.
[[raw/2026-11-08/batch-04/town-upgrade-independence.txt|Original reply]], line 1.
This adds a design rule; the earlier Town direction remains preserved.

## Summary

Level upgrades and prestige should not directly require another non-Town-Hall
building to reach a particular level or prestige. Town Hall prestige gates and
required materials are sufficient cross-building constraints.

## Findings and evidence

- Players should be able to upgrade a Smeltery without upgrading or prestiging
  the Mine.
- The need to obtain Ore from the Mine is an acceptable resource dependency.
  A direct Mine-level requirement for a Smeltery level is unwanted.
- Town Hall prestige requirements remain allowed.
- The Mine level 10 / Smeltery level 5 wording is an illustrative example,
  not selected balance values.
