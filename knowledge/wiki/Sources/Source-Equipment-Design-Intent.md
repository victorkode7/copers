## Summary

Equipment progression should combine finding items and improving rolls.
A player should use a weapon until a new option offers better stats or a unique
effect that improves how their build works.

## Findings and evidence

- Item discovery and improving rolls both contribute to the intended progression.
- The owner describes keeping a weapon until a worthwhile replacement is found.
- Better stats relative to the player's current weapon can justify replacement.
- A unique effect that improves the build can also justify replacement; this
  route is described separately from a numerical stat improvement.
