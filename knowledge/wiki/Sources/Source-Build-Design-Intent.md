# Owner's build design direction

## Provenance

Owner reply captured from this conversation on 2026-11-08 in batch-01, answering
whether players should specialize in a few abilities or spread points across
several. The UTF-8 snapshot preserves the reply verbatim with one terminal
newline. The SHA-256 above identifies the capture.

Evidence: [[raw/2026-11-08/batch-01/build-design-intent.txt|Original reply]], line 1.
This is owner direction and a reported balance concern, not a code audit or
measured build comparison. It clarifies an open question; no earlier source
is superseded.

## Summary

Players should be free to discover interesting builds through items and
abilities. Ability count is not the design target. Strong, surprising
interactions are acceptable unless they feel excessively broken.

## Findings and evidence

- The owner wants freedom to make builds and discover interesting item/ability
  interactions, without prescribing a few or many abilities.
- Interactions that seem to break the game can be acceptable within a limit:
  they should not feel overly broken. No numerical limit is given.
- The owner dislikes Piercing Arrow plus Gathering Power feeling like a direct
  upgrade in almost all builds. They suspect spell-only builds might not benefit
  much from the combination. This is their assessment, not a verified universal
  balance result.
- More specialized stats, such as the ranged damage stat on weapons, are a
  possible way to encourage focusing on one direction or another. No specific
  stat change or balance adjustment is selected.
