I think the player should be free to make any build they want. maybe they'll find some cool interaction between an item and a couple of abilities that "break" the game. I don't mind it too much as long as it doesn't feel overly broken. I'd say instead of focusing on whether they use few or many abilities I want the player to find cool builds. An example of something I don't really like at this moment is the fact that combining the piercing arrow and gathering power nodes in almost all builds is just a direct upgrade to any build you're playing(maybe not if you only run spell damage), but this may get sorted by just having more specialized stats like the ranged damage stat on weapons, making it better to focus one or the other.
