About the Game

This is an idle resource-gathering game. Chop trees, transport your logs to market, sell them for money, and use the earnings to buy better axes and automation.

Goal: Build up your woodcutting and mining operations from manual chopping to fully automated production.

How to Play
  • Chop: Click "Chop oak!" to gather oak logs manually. Each click gives you logs based on your equipped axe speed.
  • Transport: Logs move automatically from your inventory to the market at a rate set by your Caravan upgrade.
  • Sell: Click "Sell" to sell resources from the market for money. Oak = $1, Willow = $2, Stone = $1 each.
  • Upgrade: Spend money on better axes and automation to chop and mine faster.
Progression Milestones
  • $100 earned: Unlocks the Caravan (faster transport) and the Stone Axe (2x chopping speed).
  • 500 oak logs sold: Unlocks the Auto Chopper (passive log production).
  • Stone Axe purchased: Unlocks the Iron Axe (5x chopping speed).
  • Mineshaft ($1,000 + 1,000 oak): Unlocks stone mining and the Auto Miner.
